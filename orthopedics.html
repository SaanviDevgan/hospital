About the Department

The Orthopedics Department at Aurora Medical Center provides expert care for conditions affecting the bones, joints, muscles, ligaments, and tendons. Our orthopedic specialists use advanced diagnostic tools and modern surgical and non-surgical treatments to help patients recover from injuries, manage chronic conditions, and regain mobility.

Joint Replacement

Advanced hip, knee, and shoulder replacement procedures designed to relieve pain and restore movement.

Sports Injury Care

Comprehensive treatment for fractures, ligament tears, sprains, and sports-related injuries to help patients return to activity.

Spine & Bone Care

Specialized care for spinal disorders, osteoporosis, arthritis, and complex bone conditions using the latest medical techniques.

Rehabilitation Services

Personalized physical therapy and rehabilitation programs focused on improving strength, flexibility, and long-term recovery.
